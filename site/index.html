Is a local model as good as ChatGPT?

For reading and answering from your own documents, yes. For heavy reasoning on work that isn't sensitive, Otto uses cloud tools, and he always shows you which is which.

What if the server breaks?

It's covered by your monthly plan. We monitor it, keep backups, and fix or swap the machine. Your cloud tools keep working in the meantime.
